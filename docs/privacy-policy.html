Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Muonix Electrosystems LLP ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Muonix EnvGuard mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use the App:

  • Email address — used for account creation and login via Firebase Authentication
  • Sensor data — temperature, humidity, and gas level readings from your connected IoT devices
  • Device identifiers — unique IDs of IoT devices you pair with the app via QR code
  • Usage data — app interactions such as threshold settings and alert configurations

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep it secure
  • Display real-time sensor readings from your connected devices
  • Send push notifications when sensor values exceed your set thresholds
  • Store historical sensor data for your review
  • Improve the performance and reliability of the App

3. Data Storage

All data is stored securely using Firebase (Google LLC), which is hosted on Google's cloud infrastructure. Sensor data is associated with your account and accessible only to you. Firebase complies with GDPR and other major data protection regulations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Firebase / Google — for data storage, authentication, and push notifications
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our legal rights

5. Push Notifications

The App sends push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to alert you when sensor readings exceed your configured thresholds. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and Firebase security rules that ensure only authenticated users can access their own data.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for industrial and professional use and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
